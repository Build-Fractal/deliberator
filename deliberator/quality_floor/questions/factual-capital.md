# Capital of France

## Question

What is the capital of France?

## Context

This is a straightforward factual question with a single definitive answer. There is no genuine room for disagreement, alternative perspectives, or competing recommendations. Paris has been the capital of France since the 10th century, and this is not a matter of debate, interpretation, or tradeoff analysis.

This question is being used as a quality floor test case — it should produce output where agents cannot genuinely disagree, and any apparent "deliberation" would be artificial padding rather than substantive multi-perspective analysis.
