Rec 3 (add CONTRIBUTING.md to Governance's operational-guidance destinations list) would normalize CONTRIBUTING.md as an operational guidance destination, implicitly endorsing the SHOULD framing that governs it.
  - **Nature of tension**: cross-reference-coherence's Rec 3 and removal-rigor-skeptic's Rec 4 pull in different directions. Rec 3 legitimizes CONTRIBUTING.md as an operational guidance home (which the SHOULD framing is appropriate for); Rec 4 challenges the SHOULD framing and asks for MUST weight in the same document. Making CONTRIBUTING.md the named Governance destination for authoring conventions while simultaneously upgrading its normative weight back to MUST would make CONTRIBUTING.md an unusual kind of operational guidance document — one that carries constitutional obligation weight without being in the constitution.
