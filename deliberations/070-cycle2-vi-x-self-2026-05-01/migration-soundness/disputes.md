**Non-Negotiables**

1. **The mkdocs.yml nav entry for docs/output-conventions.md must be part of the v3.0.0 atomic commit (Rec 1 modified, P1).** Without this entry, docs/output-conventions.md is invisible in the built documentation site regardless of its existence on disk. Contributors reading the VI section to understand output-emitting artifact requirements will stop before L42 and proceed without awareness of the X-migration guidance. This is a navigation defect with a one-sentence fix; it should not be deferred. (migration-soundness/revision.md Rec 4; confirmed by reading CONTRIBUTING.md structure.)

1. **The specific wording of the L1589 replacement text is flexible, provided it (a) distinguishes path (c) from migrate-out as separate patterns with separate precedents and (b) names Principle XVI as the path (c) example to make the sentence navigable without requiring the reader to parse SIR comment blocks.** I prefer naming VI and X in the migrate-out clause as the most concrete and auditable form. I am flexible on whether the synthesis articulates this as rrs's explicit gate or ms's atomic commit requirement. What must be preserved: neither track's completion alone is sufficient, and the two tracks must land together.
