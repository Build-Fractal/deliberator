- **Dispute: §4.1(c) example priority — P1 vs P2**
  - **My claim**: The missing concrete examples from `skills/`, `presets/`, and `templates/` in CONTRIBUTING.md's Authoring Conventions are a P2 gap — real and should be closed, but do not make the delivered artifact non-functional or actively misleading for current contributors. *(revision, New Recommendation section)*

The three directional bullets in CONTRIBUTING.md's Authoring Conventions currently explain when scripts, YAML, and markdown are appropriate, and explain why the preference is a SHOULD rather than MUST.   - **Counter-argument to their position**: audit-faithfulness simultaneously calls the migration "substantively sound" (Position Summary) and calls the missing examples a P1 blocker. A substantively sound migration whose "practical value depends" on missing examples would be a P1 failure; a functional migration that could be enriched with examples is P2.   - **Proposed resolution path**: The synthesizer should distinguish (a) work that logically presupposes the migration decisions are unqualifiedly correct — blocked by §9.5 — from (b) governance hygiene that accurately describes what happened and what was deferred — not blocked. ### Final Position Statement

**Non-Negotiables**

1. The §9.5 supplemental blind deliberation is P0 for spec closure, and the `specs/done/` placement must be corrected — either by moving spec 070 back to `specs/` or by filing a named follow-up spec that explicitly records §9 AC #5 as unsatisfied at PR #99 closure and carries its own acceptance criteria tied to the deliberation completing. Self-attestation by the implementer is structurally inadequate for a spec whose §9 acceptance criteria require external verification; the cycle 2 blind's target-document choice makes retroactive documentation impossible rather than merely absent. *(Revised Rec 3, dispute above)*

2. Governance hygiene work that is accurate regardless of migration outcome — CONSTITUTIONAL_CONVERSATIONS.md coordinated update, XIX tracking issue, governance operational-guidance list update — is not blocked by the §9.5 gap and should proceed immediately. Conflating logically-dependent work (unqualified precedent citations) with independent governance hygiene (recording what happened and what was deferred) produces unnecessary delay on items everyone agrees are correct. The synthesizer should categorize each remediation item individually rather than blocking everything behind P0. *(Dispute 3 above)*

**Flexibility**

1. Priority and sequencing of §4.1(c) examples. I hold P2 and parallel-proceeding, but the core intent is that examples are added before spec 070 is fully closed and that the examples are reviewed against any supplemental deliberation findings before finalization. If the synthesizer adopts P2 with gap-detector's strict sequencing (deliberation first, examples second), the outcome — examples added before final closure, reviewed against any contrarian findings — is acceptable, with the cost of additional process overhead I consider unnecessary but can accommodate.

2. The scope of P0 blocking. My position distinguishes governance hygiene (not blocked) from precedent-citation work (blocked). If the synthesizer concludes that the distinction is too fine-grained to administer and prefers a bright-line rule (§9.5 gap is resolved before any new work lands on the spec 070 cluster), I can accept that formulation provided the governance hygiene items are explicitly confirmed as proceeding in parallel — i.e., the bright line applies to new work citing v3.0.0 as unqualified precedent, not to documentation-of-what-happened items that are correct independent of migration outcome.
