**Non-Negotiables**

1. **The structural definition of "plain-language explanation," the XV enforcement-mode clause, and the XV registry-emission cross-reference must be adopted as a single atomic package added to XVI's third bullet, with the structural definition understood as a logical prerequisite for the enforcement-mode clause.** Any partial adoption — enforcement sub-bullets without the structural definition, or enforcement-mode clause without the structural definition — leaves the schema lint unimplementable and the violation condition undefined. Gate-skeptic Modified Rec 4 shares this non-negotiable; WP's separable framing is the risk the synthesizer must guard against. (CPC New Rec B + Modified Recs 1/5.)

Silence is not a valid disposition; it becomes implicit acceptance of an inconsistency that both CPC (Modified Rec 6) and WP (Modified Rec 3) independently identified as unacceptable. (CPC Modified Rec 6.)
