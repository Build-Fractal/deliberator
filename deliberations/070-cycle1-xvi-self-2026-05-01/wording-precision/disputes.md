### Final Position Statement

**Non-Negotiables**:

1. The L790 self-attestation sentence must be removed before ratification. This is the unconditional prerequisite of modified Rec 1. The sentence introduces a constitutional form no other principle uses, conflicts with the body's own "evidence-pending" language at L872-876, and establishes a self-referential precedent that future principles could cite to include similar self-attestation. All three reviewers converge unconditionally; no argument for retention survived Phase 2 cross-review scrutiny.

2. Both "auditable" and "no surprise math" must be replaced with Criterion 1-compliant formulations before ratification, regardless of which paragraph-fix method the synthesizer adopts. These are P1 constitutional defects — reader-relative predicates of the same class as "user understanding" — not optional improvements. The synthesis cannot ratify XVI with these two glosses intact and maintain that the v2.6.0 amendment closes the Criterion 1 gap the SIR claims to close. (WP Revision modified Recs 2 and 5, both confirmed P1 after cross-review correction.)

3. An XV enforce-mode clause must be added to XVI's plain-language pairing requirement before ratification. The XV/XVI enforcement conflict identified by CPC is not a framing concern — it is a foreseeable production failure mode. Without an enforce-mode clause, a future implementor has affirmative text in XVI's MUST as authority for schema-level rejection of non-paired plugin output, and no text in XVI explicitly blocking that interpretation. The clause must be added; whether it arrives via targeted fix or wholesale replacement is secondary. (WP Revision new recommendation, unanimous across all three reviewers.)

**Flexibility**:

1. The method of fixing the design-intent paragraph — targeted fixes vs. GS's wholesale replacement — is WP's primary remaining dispute, but WP is willing to accept wholesale replacement provided three conditions are met: (a) "no bare numbers" or a functional equivalent is preserved in the replacement text, since GS's own analysis endorses it as passing Criterion 1; (b) the replacement text includes the XV enforce-mode clause as a sentence (not merely implied); and (c) the SIR's positioning of the design intent as a "derived property" of the structural requirements is not contradicted by the replacement text's framing. All three conditions protect legitimate constitutional content; none require preserving defective glosses.

2. The precise wording of the "no surprise math" replacement is flexible within the constraint that it must be Criterion 1-compliant and correctly scope the guarantee to post-pinning assembly. Both "assembly is deterministic given the same template and pinned parameters" (WP Rec 2 = GS Rec 6 fallback) and "byte-identical given the same template and fully-pinned parameter set" (GS Rec 1 text) satisfy this constraint. WP has no objection to "fully-pinned parameter set" if the synthesizer finds it preferable for terminological consistency with stage 3's pipeline block language, where "fully-pinned parameter set" appears verbatim. The core requirement is that the gloss not overpromise cross-run stability (the v2.3.2 clarification permits cross-run variance in parameter values) and that it name mechanically defined inputs, which both candidate formulations achieve.
