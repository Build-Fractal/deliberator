### Executive Summary

The candidate v2.6.0 Principle XVI rewrite is constitutionally sound in its central maneuver: replacing the epistemically unverifiable headline ("the user MUST understand what is being optimized, even without understanding the math") with three structurally verifiable requirements (parameter pinning, shape determinism, plain-language output pairing) as the load-bearing claim, then repositioning the original framing as design intent the structural requirements achieve. All three headline requirements map unambiguously to body elements, the compound RFC 2119 MUST distributes correctly across the conjunction, and the "byte-identical" scope is correctly conditioned on post-pinning assembly throughout. No internal contradiction with the unchanged v2.3.2 Clarification body is introduced.

Two wording defects require correction. First, the plain-language gloss "template shapes are stable (no surprise math)" at L788 overpromises: the v2.3.2 Clarification explicitly permits cross-run variance in resolved parameter values (L848-852), meaning two separate invocations can produce mathematically distinct objective functions. "No surprise math" does not accurately characterize a guarantee that is conditional on parameter pinning having already occurred. Second, the self-attestation sentence at L790 — "Each requirement is mechanically verifiable, falsifiable, and distinct from existing principles" — is criteria-compliance metadata that belongs in the SIR header comment (where it already appears at L34-82), not in the principle body. It is a net-new sentence not in any prior version of XVI, it creates an unusual constitutional precedent, and it conflicts with the Enforcement sub-bullet's honest "evidence-pending" caveat at L872-876 for the pinning discipline specifically.

The most important recommendation is to remove the self-attestation sentence at L790 from the principle body, and to replace "no surprise math" with a conditional gloss that accurately characterizes what shape determinism actually guarantees.

### Missed Opportunities

- **"No surprise math" gloss actively misleads on the scope of the guarantee**: The design-intent paragraph at L788 uses "template shapes are stable (no surprise math)" as the plain-language gloss for shape determinism. The v2.3.2 Clarification at L848-852 is explicit: "cross-run variance in resolved parameter *values* is acceptable (e.g., two `/conversus mode` invocations on the same `problem.md` MAY produce different `objective.yml` files)." Two invocations on identical input can produce different objective functions (different pinned parameter values → different assembled math). A user relying on "no surprise math" as a behavioral guarantee will be surprised by this permitted cross-run variance. The gloss should be conditioned on parameter pinning: something like "assembly is reproducible given the same parameters" captures what the guarantee actually covers. **Impact: high** — a falsifiable inaccuracy in the principle body that generates compliance disputes when cross-run variance is observed.

- **Self-attestation sentence at L790 is SIR metadata misplaced in the principle body**: "Each requirement is mechanically verifiable, falsifiable, and distinct from existing principles" (L790) is a criteria-compliance attestation. The SIR header comment at L34-82 already contains the full three-criterion self-assessment. No other principle in I-XXVIII contains such a self-attestation in its normative body text — XXVIII, the most recent addition, places its criteria compliance exclusively in the SIR (L134-148). Moving the attestation into the body creates an unusual precedent: the principle must remain factually true as enforcement mechanisms evolve, but the Enforcement sub-bullet at L872-876 already acknowledges "evidence-pending" for the pinning discipline's contract test. The sentence is thus both novel constitutional form and internally inconsistent with the body's honest caveat. **Impact: high** — scope creep beyond the SIR-stated amendment boundary; establishes a problematic precedent for future principles.

- **"Template shapes are stable" mischaracterizes what shape determinism guarantees** (L788): Templates can and do change — spec 013 defines the template definitions stage 1 parses (L882-884), and constitutional amendments are the mechanism for template evolution. "Template shapes are stable" reads as a claim about template mutability, not about assembly determinism. The accurate gloss is about the assembly process given a fixed template: "assembly structure is deterministic given the same template and pinned parameters." **Impact: medium** — a future spec 013 amendment (a legitimate template change) may be challenged under the "stable" gloss.

- **Enforcement and Falsification sub-bullets at L872-879 cover only parameter pinning, not shape determinism or plain-language pairing**: The design-intent paragraph asserts "each requirement is mechanically verifiable" (L789-790), but the body demonstrates this only for parameter pinning via the Enforcement and Falsification sub-bullets (L872-879). Shape determinism and plain-language pairing have no corresponding enforcement sub-bullets in the body — their verifiability paths (parity test, schema lint) are documented in the SIR at L38-45, not in the principle text. A reader relying solely on the principle body cannot verify the "mechanically verifiable" assertion for requirements (2) and (3). **Impact: medium** — the body's failure to ground all three verifiability claims creates a gap that the self-attestation sentence at L790 then asserts without support.

- **"Auditable" in the parameter-pinning gloss requires body knowledge to interpret** (L787): "user-provided parameters stay pinned (auditable)" tells a reader there is something to audit but not what or where. The body provides this context at L860-862 (`SourceProvenance.filled_by`, `objective.yml`). Since plain-language glosses are meant to orient readers without requiring them to have read the body, "persisted to `objective.yml`, not re-resolved" is more orienting than "auditable" alone and remains equally concise. **Impact: low** — the gloss functions less effectively as a standalone orientation aid.

- **The design-intent paragraph conflates two distinct purposes in one block** (L785-790): The first 1.5 sentences (L785-789) are explanatory prose positioning the design intent as a derived property. The final sentence (L790) is criteria-compliance metadata. Grouping them in a single paragraph makes the paragraph's status ambiguous — is it normative text, explanatory commentary, or amendment-rationale metadata? Future reviewers applying the principle in isolation may treat the self-attestation sentence as a normative claim they must evaluate, not as metadata about the amendment. **Impact: low** — structural clarity for future readers.
