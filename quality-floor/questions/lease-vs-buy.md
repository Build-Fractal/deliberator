# Lease vs Buy Decision

## Question

Should I lease or buy a mid-size sedan if I drive 12,000 miles/year and plan to keep it 5 years?

## Context

I am a suburban commuter in the mid-Atlantic region (moderate climate, mix of highway and city driving). My daily round-trip commute is 35 miles, and I use the car for weekend errands and occasional road trips to family 200 miles away, totaling approximately 12,000 miles per year. I plan to have this car for 5 years before re-evaluating.

My budget for a monthly payment is $400-500. I have $5,000 available for a down payment and my credit score is 760. I am looking at mid-size sedans in the $32,000-38,000 MSRP range (e.g., Honda Accord, Toyota Camry, Mazda 6 class). I have no strong brand preference but value reliability and low maintenance costs.

I am not mechanically inclined and would rely on dealer or shop service for all maintenance. I keep my cars clean but am realistic about wear — I have two young children who will inevitably spill things. I do not need the latest model every 3 years for professional appearance or personal satisfaction; I am primarily optimizing for total cost of ownership over the 5-year period.

## Decision Criteria

- Total cost of ownership over 5 years
- Monthly cash flow impact
- Flexibility if circumstances change (job relocation, family size change)
- Maintenance and repair risk exposure
- End-of-term value and options
